Motor Spin Test

Open-loop: holds one motor at a fixed PWM (no PID, no target) so you can time output-shaft rotations by hand (mark the wheel, use a stopwatch) - motor-shaft speed alone can't reveal two motors having different gearbox ratios (same free-spin speed can still gear down differently). Runs up to 60s, or until Stop.

"Run Search" generates a random solvable maze and replays the robot's actual exploration algorithm (maze.cpp) against it, storing the discovered wall map here in the browser.
